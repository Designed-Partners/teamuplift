Your impact

Where your support goes.

Every dollar helps ensure a young person’s potential is never limited by their circumstances.

  • Scholarships

    Removing financial barriers to higher education for students in our community.

  • Mentorship

    Connecting young people, with a focus on Black men and boys, to mentors who invest in their growth.

  • Health & Wellness

    Programming and events like the Bulldog Run that build healthy habits and resilience.

  • STEM & Financial Literacy

    Hands-on learning, career exposure, and practical money skills for a stable future.

  • Community & Civic Initiatives

    Neighborhood clean ups and nonpartisan voter registration and education.
