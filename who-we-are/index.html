Who we are

Empowering LA’s youth to thrive.

A Los Angeles nonprofit giving young people the skills, exposure, and support they need to build the futures they deserve.

Our story

Eighty years of community service, carried forward.

Team Uplift Coalition, Inc. is a nonprofit organization dedicated to empowering youth in Los Angeles with the skills, exposure, and support they need to thrive.

We are proud to partner with Lambda Omicron Chapter of Omega Psi Phi Fraternity, Inc., chartered in 1946 and one of the fraternity’s founding chapters in the 12th District, bringing eight decades of community service experience in South Los Angeles to our mission.

Our mission

Holistic development for every young person.

We work to empower youth in Los Angeles with life skills, exposure to diverse career paths, and comprehensive STEM education.

We foster holistic development in mental, spiritual, and physical well-being, all while promoting financial literacy, so that a young person’s potential is never limited by their circumstances.
